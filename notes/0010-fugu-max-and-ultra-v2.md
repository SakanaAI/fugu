Two new Fugu models, and Fugu Max is the new default

The /model picker now offers six models. Two are new: Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2.0. The list is ordered Fugu Max, Fugu Ultra v2.0, Fugu, Fugu Ultra v1.1, Fugu Ultra v1.0, Fugu Cyber.

Fugu Ultra v2.0 is the unversioned "fugu-ultra" entry, shown on its own again. This supersedes the earlier note "Fugu Ultra is now version-pinned", which said that entry was no longer shown on its own: selecting it always gives you the current Ultra release, while fugu-ultra-v1.1 and fugu-ultra-v1.0 are unchanged and still pin you to one specific release.

Nothing is required on your part, but the launch default has changed: new sessions now start on Fugu Max, at the same high reasoning effort as before, instead of Fugu. Your configs and API key are unchanged, and /model still switches you to any model in the list at any time — pick Fugu if you want the previous default back.
